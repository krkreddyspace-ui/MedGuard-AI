## Photography Best Practices for Blister Packaging
- **Lighting**: Use diffuse, front-facing light. Avoid direct flash which reflects off shiny foil.
- **Framing**: Keep the commercial brand name or active ingredient text filling at least 50% of the frame.
- **Orientation**: Hold the strip horizontally and flat against a neutral surface.
- **Resolution**: Standard smartphone camera resolution (1080p or higher) is ideal.
